## 5. Source conflicts and numbering decisions required

1. **Parallel structures:** Beginner appears to merge a beat-production sequence with an audio-engineering sequence. Intermediate and Advanced show the same pattern. Human reviewers must decide whether these are duplicate generated curricula, parallel tracks, or one intentionally combined course.
2. **Duplicate numbering:** Levels 1-4 all contain duplicate source module numbers. These cannot map to unique `level_module_number` values without a canonical decision.
3. **Missing numbers:** Intermediate Module 11 is absent; Advanced Modules 11-12 are absent; Master Modules 11-18 are absent before the jump to 19.
4. **Late appended structures:** Advanced Modules 13-18 and Master Modules 19-25 appear as appended generated blocks and may represent a different curriculum pass.
5. **Capstones:** Master Module 10 and Master Module 25 are both capstones. Their relationship and final sequencing require explicit approval.
6. **Tutor divergence:** The Tutor PDF presents clean 15-topic sequences per level, including topics not named in the Program. These are coaching groupings only and cannot resolve Program numbering.
7. **Cross-course scope:** Beat production, harmony, jazz, blues, songwriting/arrangement, DJ performance, artistic voice, dance/artist studies, and film/game audio may belong partly to other JPAC courses. They remain source evidence but require **SCOPE REVIEW**.

## 6. Interleaved/generated curriculum assessment

The Program appears to contain at least two interleaved generations in each level:

- a concise production-oriented sequence emphasizing Cakewalk, beats, synthesis, MIDI, arrangement, genres, and artistic production; and
- an engineering-oriented sequence emphasizing acoustics, microphones, signal flow, recording, EQ, compression, mixing, mastering, studio operations, clients, and delivery.

The late Advanced 13-18 and Master 19-25 blocks may be third-pass extensions. They should not be silently discarded, compressed, or assigned new numbers. A human canonical map must label every raw slot as **KEEP**, **MERGE INTO**, **HOLD**, or **OUT OF AUDIO ENGINEERING SCOPE** before SQL.
