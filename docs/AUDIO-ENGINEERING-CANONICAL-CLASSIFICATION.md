## 1. Source authority recap

This classification applies the source rules recorded in `docs/AUDIO-ENGINEERING-FULL-PROGRAM-SOURCE-MAP.md`:

- The Audio Engineering Program PDF controls evidenced structure and content.
- The Audio Engineering Tutor PDF is guidance-only for Aria coaching and cannot add, remove, reorder, or rename Program modules.
- MasterGrid, Video Production, Music Production/Songwriting, Artist Development, Career Paths, and older tracker structures are not Audio Engineering structural authority.
- Every raw Program heading remains traceable below, including duplicates, missing-number sequences, late appended blocks, and cross-course material.

Classification meanings:

- **KEEP** - clearly Audio Engineering and recommended as a standalone rollout module.
- **MERGE** - valid Audio Engineering content that substantially overlaps a kept module.
- **HOLD** - potentially valid but requires teacher, source, safety, rights, tool, or scope review.
- **OUT OF SCOPE** - more clearly belongs to another JPAC course or is not appropriate as an Audio Engineering module now.

## 9. Capstone decision

- **Beginner showcase:** canonical B12, Beginner Mixdown Showcase.
- **Intermediate showcase:** canonical I12, Acoustic Treatment & Studio Readiness, with a documented room/session plan and controlled listening demonstration.
- **Advanced capstone/showcase:** canonical A12, Full Band Recording Showcase. It integrates microphone selection, routing, gain staging, editing, mixing, consent, safety, and professional delivery. A supplied multitrack/stem alternative is mandatory.
- **Master capstone/final evaluation:** raw Master Module 25, `Capstone: Complete Album Production`, becomes canonical M12.
- Raw Master Module 10, `Capstone: Mix and Master an Album`, is merged into M12 as staged mix/master milestones and evidence requirements. It is not a second standalone capstone.

This resolves the competing capstone signals without fabricating or discarding their assessed content.

## 10. Review flags

- **SCOPE REVIEW:** cross-course or specialization boundaries.
- **TOOL/ACCESSIBILITY REVIEW:** DAWs, plugins, hardware, Atmos, studio access, and equivalent evidence.
- **AI/ETHICS REVIEW:** AI mastering, generation, restoration, provenance, disclosure, bias, consent, and rights.
- **BUSINESS/LEGAL REVIEW:** clients, contracts, credits, mentoring, studio operations, distribution, and confidentiality.
- **RIGHTS/DISTRIBUTION REVIEW:** samples, remixes, reference tracks, artists, streaming, albums, film/game audio, and public delivery.
- **MEDIA NEEDS REVIEW:** every overview video, especially `None` contradictions, fake IDs, placeholders, and repeated links.
- **NEEDS CATALOG REVIEW:** every JPAC-labelled or external tool; no binding is authorized.

All canonical modules also require safe-listening language, hearing protection, reasonable monitoring levels, breaks, and accessible no-hardware/supplied-material alternatives.

## 12. Do Not Roll Out

- fabricated modules or numbers introduced only to fill gaps;
- Tutor PDF-only modules or Tutor numbering;
- HOLD or OUT OF SCOPE headings without separate approval;
- activated media rows or media versions;
- bound tool, Lab, plugin, DAW, Atmos, or external-service records;
- Google Classroom workflow instead of JPAC-native submission;
- mandatory Cakewalk, CapCut, named DAWs, plugins, external accounts, studio hardware, or immersive systems;
- fake or placeholder video URLs, including `abc123xyz` and `REAL_VIDEO_ID`;
- repeated generic links as active instructional media;
- cross-course content without ownership approval;
- public distribution, client work, copyrighted sampling, remixing, or AI use without required review;
- published or student-active curriculum.
